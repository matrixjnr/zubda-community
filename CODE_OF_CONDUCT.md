# Community conduct

Be respectful, constructive and patient with people of different backgrounds and experience. Discuss the technical question; avoid personal attacks, harassment, discrimination, spam and unsolicited promotion. Never publish customer information or credentials.

Maintainers may edit or remove unsafe or off-topic content, close discussions and restrict participation when necessary. Report conduct concerns privately through [Zubda contact](https://www.zubda.co/contact/). Report vulnerabilities to security@zubda.co.
